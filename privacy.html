Your privacy

Privacy Policy

What this website collects, what you choose to share, and how it is used and protected.

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you use this website or contact Maxwell Kingsbury, a licensed independent insurance agent (Florida License # G365332, NPN 22312975), who operates this website under the name Maxwell Helps ("I," "we" or "us"), and how it is used.

What this website collects

This website has no contact forms or user accounts. It uses one advertising tool, the Meta Pixel, described below. Specifically:

  • Plan-fit questions. Your answers stay in your own browser. They are not stored or sent to anyone, and they disappear when you leave the page.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, under GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • Meta Pixel. We use the Meta Pixel (from Meta Platforms, which runs Facebook and Instagram) to measure our ads. When you visit a page, your browser sends Meta the page address, your IP address, browser details and a Meta cookie. When you click “Start intake form,” it also tells Meta that the button was clicked. We do not send Meta your plan-fit answers or anything you type into the intake form. Meta may use this information to show and measure ads, under Meta’s own privacy policy. You can limit this in your Facebook ad preferences or by blocking third-party cookies in your browser.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and browser details to Google to deliver them. Google's privacy policy applies to that request.

Information you choose to share

If you call, email or complete the intake form, we receive the information you provide. That may include your name, contact details, date of birth, ZIP code, current coverage, doctors and prescriptions.

The intake form is hosted by HealthSherpa, a third-party enrollment platform. Information you enter there is collected by HealthSherpa and shared with us, and HealthSherpa's own privacy policy also applies.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and compare the plans we offer against your needs
  • To help you apply for or enroll in a plan, when you ask us to
  • To follow up with you about your request, and for annual reviews if you would like them
  • To meet legal, regulatory and carrier requirements, including Medicare (CMS) rules. For example, calls about Medicare Advantage and Part D plans may be recorded as CMS requires.

When we share it

We share your information only as needed to help you. That includes insurance carriers when you apply for coverage, enrollment platforms, and the organizations that support our agency work, such as a marketing organization we are contracted with. We may also share information when the law or a regulator requires it.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with others for their own marketing.

How we contact you

We contact you only about the request you made or the service you asked for. You can ask us to stop calling, texting or emailing you at any time, and we will honor that request.

Security and retention

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. We keep it only as long as needed to serve you and to meet record-keeping rules that apply to licensed agents, carriers and Medicare.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Some records must be kept for legal or regulatory reasons, and we will explain if that applies. Contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above.
