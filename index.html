Got questions?

We have great answers

What if I'm already on Medicare?

We can still help at no cost. We'll review your current coverage, check your doctors and prescriptions, and compare it with other plans we offer.

Do you charge for your time?

No. Insurance carriers pay agents a commission when you enroll, so there is no charge to you for our help.

What are "lifetime penalties"?

If you don't enroll in Part B or Part D when first eligible (and lack other qualifying coverage), a permanent late-enrollment penalty can be added to your premium.

Could I lose my doctor?

Some plans use provider networks. Before you enroll, we check your doctors and prescriptions against each plan's network and drug list. Networks and drug lists can change, so it's worth rechecking every year.

What do you offer?

We offer Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement (Medigap), Part D prescription drug plans, and dental, vision & hearing coverage.
